ManuCare CI Saver

Comprehensive critical illness protection for every stage of life.

Plan Overview

ManuCare CI Saver provides coverage for 36 critical illnesses across early, intermediate, and advanced stages. Designed for individuals seeking robust protection with flexible premium payment terms.

Coverage & Benefits

  • Sum Assured: SGD 150,000
  • Payout: 100% lump sum upon diagnosis
  • Covers 36 conditions including early-stage cancer and heart attack
  • 5% no-claim bonus accrued annually (up to 25%)
  • Return of premium if no claim by end of policy term

Policy Terms

  • Coverage term: 20 years or to age 70
  • Waiting period: 90 days for most conditions
  • Premium frequency: Annual or monthly

Exclusions

  • Pre-existing conditions
  • Conditions caused by war or nuclear events
  • Self-inflicted injuries
  • 90-day waiting period applies
